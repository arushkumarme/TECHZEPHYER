Hall-name verification note

The official Hall of Residence overview says there are four halls (Ganga, Mahanadi, Brahmaputra, Subarnarekha) with capacity about 2,000; the same page's contact list also names Rushikulya and Sangam HR. The Students Life page has a different/older count and mentions A.N. Khosla Hall outside the permanent campus. Because public pages are internally inconsistent, this map lists the names but only pins MHR, BHR and RHR where they appear on the supplied campus road map.
